We are pleased to announce the approval of the INA study, a collaborative research initiative between Utrecht University and [Diponegoro University](https://undip.ac.id/language/en/home). This partnership brings together complementary expertise and establishes a foundation for joint research efforts aimed at advancing our understanding of health and disease through international scientific collaboration. Special congratulation to our PhD student Yunita Rahmawati for all her efforts in this!
